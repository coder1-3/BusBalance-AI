BusBalance AI is a concept for making public bus services more responsive to passenger demand. It explores how information about when and where people travel can help public transport operators plan bus schedules and allocate buses more effectively.

## The problem
Passenger demand changes throughout the day and across different routes. A fixed bus schedule may not always reflect those changes. As a result, some services may become overcrowded while others may have relatively low passenger demand.

---

## The idea
<img src="static/images/Smarter Bus Scheduling with AI-2.png" align="right" width="350" style="margin-left: 20px; border-radius: 8px;">

BusBalance AI aims to support more demand-aware public bus scheduling by:

- **Understanding passenger demand** — identifying patterns across routes, days, and time periods.
- **Anticipating busy periods** — highlighting routes and times when passenger demand may be higher.
- **Supporting better schedules** — exploring how bus frequency and allocation could be adjusted to match demand.
- **Comparing alternatives** — making it easier to examine an existing schedule alongside a proposed one.

## Potential benefits
<img src="static/images/BusBalance AI_ Smarter Transit Tonight-3.png" align="right" width="350" style="margin-left: 20px; border-radius: 8px;">

- Better alignment between bus services and passenger demand.
- Earlier visibility into routes and periods that may experience overcrowding.
- Identification of services that may be underused.
- More informed decisions about the use of available buses.
- Potential improvements in passenger waiting times and operating efficiency.

> **Note:** These are potential benefits, not guaranteed results. Outcomes depend on data quality, local travel patterns, operating constraints, and decisions made by transport authorities.
